# Problem Statement and Scope

## Problem statement

A beginner using myASNB may need to compare fund objectives, price type, risk information, eligibility, and fees before deciding what to investigate further. ASNB publishes these details, but a new investor may find it difficult to weigh several criteria when reading different fund descriptions. The challenge is not a proven failure of the current application; it is a design question about whether a more direct comparison and planning view would improve understanding. This matters because unclear assumptions or overlooked charges could affect how confidently a person evaluates the alternatives.

## Evidence and design assumption

ASNB distinguishes between fixed-price funds, generally priced at RM1 per unit, and variable-price funds, whose unit values depend on net asset value. It also directs investors to prospectuses, Product Highlights Sheets, and Fund Fact Sheets for risks and charges. The myASNB platform already includes an Investor Suitability Assessment and fund suggestions, while Ria provides automated investment services. Compare and Plan would extend this support rather than replace it.

Whether a separate comparison view would help beginners remains a design hypothesis. It should be validated through observation, a usability exercise, or a credible study before being described as a confirmed user problem. No interviews or user testing are claimed in this repository.

## Users and stakeholders

- **Primary users:** new adult investors, including students and young employees saving towards a goal.
- **Secondary users:** returning investors comparing alternatives.
- **Stakeholders:** ASNB content and data owners, UX designers, accessibility specialists, compliance and security reviewers, and potential users.

## In scope

- Compare two or three selected ASNB funds using matching, source-linked, dated fields.
- Filter funds using published eligibility information and selected fund attributes.
- Explore an illustrative savings scenario using editable contribution, period, and hypothetical-rate assumptions.
- Display plain-language explanations, risk notices, source links, and missing-data warnings.
- Keep the final decision with the user.

## Out of scope

- Sign in to a real ASNB account or access private customer data.
- Execute purchases, redemptions, transfers, or fund switches.
- Predict live fund performance, guarantee returns, or provide personalised financial advice.
- Replace the Investor Suitability Assessment, Ria, Auto Labur, or other existing ASNB services.
- Claim findings from interviews, surveys, or usability tests that have not occurred.

## Proposed evaluation

A later low-fidelity test could use fictional fund profiles and ask five beginners to compare two options, locate fee information, and change a monthly savings amount. Task completion, time, and points of confusion could be recorded. This is a proposed method only; no results are reported.
